Consumer Financial Protection Bureau Guidance on Complaint Handling

Financial institutions must establish and maintain an effective process for receiving, investigating, and responding to consumer complaints. Institutions shall acknowledge receipt of a written complaint within 15 business days.

Institutions must maintain a written record of each complaint received, including the date of receipt, the nature of the complaint, and the resolution provided to the consumer.

If a complaint cannot be resolved within 60 days, the institution must provide the consumer with a written explanation of the delay and an estimated timeline for resolution.

Institutions must not retaliate against a consumer for filing a complaint, and must ensure that complaint-handling staff are not the same individuals whose conduct is the subject of the complaint.
